About ReviewWise

ReviewWise is an AI-assisted credibility checking tool designed to identify language patterns that may indicate clickbait, exaggerated claims, excessive punctuation, emotional language, or other suspicious signals.

Important

The credibility score is a risk indicator. It does not prove that information is true or false. Important claims should still be verified using reliable sources.
