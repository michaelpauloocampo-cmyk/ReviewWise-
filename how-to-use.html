How to Use

A quick guide before you start checking content.

1
Add your content

Type or paste text, tap the Speak button to speak it, or use the + button to scan a photo, video, or upload a PDF, DOCX, or TXT file.

2
Review extracted text

If you scanned an image, video, or file, check the extracted text and edit it before analyzing.

3
Analyze Content

Tap "Analyze Content" to get a credibility score along with the patterns behind it.

4
Read the results

Check the score, the detected patterns, and the highlighted text to see exactly why something was flagged.

5
Revisit anytime

Open the sidebar menu to view your Check History or come back to this guide.

Remember

The credibility score is a risk indicator, not proof. Always verify important claims with reliable sources.
